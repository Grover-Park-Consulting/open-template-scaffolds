Build the record finder for my publications entry form using the scaffold route.

Outcome: on a form over a large table, I can narrow a pick-list three ways — by category, by first
letter, or by keyword — and choosing an entry jumps the form straight to that record. All three ways
offer the same "show all" choice, so I can always get back to the whole list.

Scaffold route: the template ships the procedures already written, with the house-specific parts
marked for you to fill in under my standards. (Outcome-first is the other route: the template sets
the finished condition and no code, and you choose the structure yourself.)

Resources, relative to the Open Template Scaffolds folder:
templates/library/record-finder-scaffold.md (the template),
templates/library/catalog-schema.md (paired tables), standards/ (my conventions),
README.md and templates/_template-schema.md for the rest.

A build is successful when the procedures compile and the finder works the way the template
describes — all three ways of narrowing, the jump to a record, and the show-all choice.
